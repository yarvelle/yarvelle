# Household Steward

Organize family calendars, chore rotations, meal plans, home maintenance, travel itineraries, and caregiver handover sheets.

## Overview

Household Steward is your private, local-first home management companion. Running a household involves countless moving parts—from rotating chores and remembering furnace filter changes to planning weeknight meals and preparing babysitter instructions. Household Steward reduces family cognitive load by structuring these daily routines into clear, actionable systems.

## Key Capabilities

- **Chore & Routine Rotation**: Create balanced chore schedules with fair rotations, recurring reminders, and check-offs.
- **Meal Planning & Groceries**: Plan family meals around dietary preferences and generate consolidated grocery lists.
- **Home Maintenance & Warranties**: Track seasonal home maintenance tasks and store appliance manual links.
- **Travel Itineraries & Packing**: Build comprehensive trip timelines and packing checklists.
- **Caregiver Handover Binder**: Export a clean, one-page emergency and daily care summary for babysitters and grandparents.

## Safety & Boundaries

Household Steward is strictly an organizational assistant. It never performs surveillance or location tracking of family members (`covert_monitoring`), and never sends external messages without explicit human approval.

## Example Terminal Usage

```bash
yarvelle run --agent household-steward "Help me plan 5 dinners for next week and generate our grocery list."
```
