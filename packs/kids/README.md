# Yarvelle Kids Pack

Child education navigation, homework assistance, reading logs, school forms, extracurricular activities, and household coordination pack for Yarvelle.

## Child Safety & Privacy Boundaries
- Yarvelle strictly enforces COPPA under-13 privacy with local-first processing.
- Academic integrity: explanations and practice quizzes only, no essay writing or homework completion.
- Anti-ranking: no percentile scoring or competitive comparisons.
- Non-diagnostic: no learning disability diagnoses (ADHD, dyslexia, ASD).
- Drafts only: school forms and permission slips require human guardian review and signature.
- No covert monitoring or hidden surveillance of family members.
