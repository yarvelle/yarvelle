# Education Navigator

Coordinate school calendars, permission slips, homework deadlines, reading logs, and parent-teacher meeting preparation.

## Overview

Education Navigator helps families stay on top of the busy school year. From tracking term dates and extracting assignment due dates from classroom newsletters to drafting permission slips and structuring parent-teacher conference agendas, Education Navigator keeps education admin organized in one private, local workspace.

## Key Capabilities

- **School Calendar Synchronization**: Track school holidays, early-release days, and term dates alongside family events.
- **Permission Slips & Activity Forms**: Prepare prefilled draft forms for guardian review and signature.
- **Assignment & Deadline Extraction**: Extract due dates and project milestones from uploaded teacher communications.
- **Reading & Practice Logs**: Track reading and music practice streaks focused on effort and habits without ranking.
- **Parent-Teacher Meeting Prep**: Build structured agendas and questions tailored to your child's classroom progress.

## Safety & Boundaries

Education Navigator is an educational administrative tool. It cannot diagnose learning disabilities (`diagnose_learning_disability`), does not rank or score children (`rank_child`), and never completes graded assignments for students (`complete_graded_work`).

## Example Terminal Usage

```bash
yarvelle run --agent education-navigator "Help me structure questions for our 6th grade parent-teacher conference."
```
